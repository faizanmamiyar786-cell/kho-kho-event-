01 / THE CHAMPIONSHIP

ROOTED IN INDIA. BUILT FOR THE FUTURE.

MORE THAN
A GAME.

The Kho Kho Championship brings athletes, institutions and sports lovers together to celebrate one of India's most exciting traditional games.

Built around speed, agility, teamwork and tactical thinking, the championship aims to create a competitive platform for emerging talent while inspiring a new generation to discover and play Kho Kho.
